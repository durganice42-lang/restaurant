Our Philosophy

A Little Story of Sweetness

Founded in the heart of Provence in 2009, Maison de la Douceur was born out of an unyielding passion for traditional French pastry craftsmanship.

We believe that baking is a form of poetry. Every morning at 4:00 AM, our bakers begin kneading organic flour, whipping Normandy butter, and simmering fresh fruits into small-batch conserves. We source single-origin vanilla beans from Madagascar and cocoa from ethical fair-trade farms.

“We don’t just bake pastries; we create sweet memories that linger on the palate and warm the heart.”

— Chef Élodie Laurent, Founder
